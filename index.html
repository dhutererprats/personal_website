Visibility and prediction limits

Rise and set use the geometric horizon. Bearings run clockwise from true north: N 0°, E 90°, S 180°, W 270°. Visible intervals require elevation of at least 10°, the Sun at least 6° below your horizon, and a sufficiently sunlit station. A pass can fade into Earth’s shadow before it sets.

A dark sky alone does not mean the station is visible; a station can also be sunlit during the middle of the night. Clouds, local terrain, light pollution, station orientation, and brightness are not modeled. Seven-day estimates use SGP4 and should be rechecked near the pass, especially after maneuvers. Elements older than three days disable predictions.
