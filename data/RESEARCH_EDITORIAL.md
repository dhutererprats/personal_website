# Research Page Editorial Policy

Updated at the author's request on 2026-10-07.

Research is organized by topic, with each paper appearing once below its topic.
Describe the scientific problem and formulation from the verified PDF abstract
and introduction. An individual result is not a substitute for a paper's scope.
Do not reintroduce oversized metrics or the repeated "Main takeaway / What is
new / Outcome" template. Do not describe an adapted policy architecture as a
new architecture.

Give every paper a visible preview from its own verified figure beside its
citation and scope description. Keep larger figures and videos in native
disclosures. For unpublished work without public figures, explicitly label a
related published-work visual; never imply it is a result from the manuscript.
Captions identify the experiment and the exact source figure/page, without
asserting that a selected plot represents the paper's principal contribution.
Preserve figure provenance in `research-papers.json`; its numerical records are
audit evidence, not headline copy. Conference and journal versions remain
distinct, and the GN&C 2027 papers remain In progress per the author's override.

Use unframed topic sections and readable bibliographic rows, with system
typography, immediate press feedback, keyboard-accessible disclosures, stable
media dimensions, and reduced-motion/transparency/contrast preferences.
